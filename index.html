How we work

Built on evidence, told by people

  • The oldest source, not the most famous. Every historical claim is traced to a primary source or named scholarship.
  • Real archives first. Photographs, documents, icons and maps, credited with their licence.
  • Illustration is labelled. It never stands in for sacred or historical evidence.
