Why are there so many Greeks in Tarpon Springs, Florida?

In 1905 John Cocoris recruited Greek sponge divers to work the sponge beds off the Florida coast, bringing diving suits and methods the local industry had never used. Divers came first from Aegina and Hydra, then from the Dodecanese islands of Kalymnos, Symi and Halki.

The community built a church, a sponge exchange and a Greek town around the docks. Today Tarpon Springs has the highest percentage of Greek-Americans of any city in the United States, and its Epiphany cross dive is famous across the country.
