Who were the Vallahades?

The Vallahades were Greek-speaking Muslims of western Macedonia, around Anaselitsa and Grevena. Their ancestors converted to Islam under Ottoman rule, but the community kept the Greek language and much of its Greek culture for generations.

In the population exchange between Greece and Turkey in 1923, people were sorted by religion, not language. The Vallahades were classified as Muslims and sent to Turkey.
