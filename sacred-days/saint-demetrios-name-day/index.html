When is Saint Demetrios name day?

This year: Monday, October 26, 2026

October 26. On the calendar followed by the Church of Greece, the feast of Saint Demetrios (Άγιος Δημήτριος), the saint of Thessaloniki, falls on October 26 every year.

Who celebrates

People named Dimitris (Δημήτρης), Dimitrios (Δημήτριος) and Dimitra (Δήμητρα) celebrate their name day on the feast of Saint Demetrios.

Old Calendar communities

Churches that keep the Old (Julian) Calendar celebrate the same feast 13 days later, on November 8 of the civil calendar.

What to say

The usual wish on a name day is Χρόνια πολλά (Chronia polla), “many years”.
