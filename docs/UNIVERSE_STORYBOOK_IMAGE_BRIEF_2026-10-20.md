# Storybook of memories: image brief for ChatGPT (PROPOSAL, 2026-10-20)
Prototype section `#storybook` on `/prototype/universe/` (design branch only). Pages open for published films and stay locked for the next film. Claude built the page mechanics only and made no artwork.

## Rules
- Illustrations come only from ChatGPT's workflow or the rights-cleared archive. Claude does not create them.
- Saint Demetrios story, credits and artwork stay held until the Oct 26, 2026 premiere. The locked page shows only the public title and date.
- Labelled "Illustration" where AI-made. No identifiable real people, no family likeness, no island names, no text or flags in the image.
- One image per page. Not collages. 16:10 crop must keep the subject centered (safe area: central 60%).

## Mechanic
Scroll-snap pages with Previous and Turn the page buttons. Open pages show Memory unlocked. Locked pages show the opening date. Unlocking is a deliberate content change at release, not a clock. Motion is gentle and off under reduced motion.
