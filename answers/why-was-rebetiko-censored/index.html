Why was rebetiko censored?

Rebetiko grew up in the ports, working-class neighbourhoods and refugee communities of early twentieth-century Greece. Its songs were about love, hardship, prison, drugs, poverty and longing.

In the 1930s the dictatorship of Ioannis Metaxas imposed censorship on Greek music. Lyrics and recordings could be censored, and elements the authorities linked to an eastern or underworld identity came under pressure. Musicians adapted, and the music survived.
