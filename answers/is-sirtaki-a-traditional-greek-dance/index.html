Is sirtaki a traditional Greek dance?

Not in the way most people think. Sirtaki was created for the film Zorba the Greek (1964). Giorgos Provias handled the choreography, drawing on Greek dances that already existed.

Anthony Quinn later recalled that an injured foot left him unable to do the jumping steps, so he dragged the foot instead. The dance became famous with the film and its music by Mikis Theodorakis.

Sirtaki isn't ancient, but it has become part of Greece's international cultural identity. Greece itself has many older regional dances, from tsamiko on the mainland to pentozali in Crete and sousta on the islands.
