What is a Greek spoon sweet?

A spoon sweet, glyko tou koutaliou in Greek, is fruit preserved in thick syrup. It is served on a small spoon, traditionally to every guest who arrives at a Greek home, as a gesture of hospitality.

Almost any fruit can be used: sour cherries, quince, figs, citrus peel, even young walnuts. For many families it is a taste of childhood, kept in a jar in the cupboard for visitors.
