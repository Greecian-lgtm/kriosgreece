Who was Athanasios Diakos?

Athanasios Diakos was a Greek commander of the War of Independence. He had trained as a deacon, which is where the name Diakos comes from, before becoming a klepht, one of the mountain fighters who resisted Ottoman rule.

In April 1821 he held the bridge of Alamana, near Thermopylae, against a much larger Ottoman force. Captured after the battle, he was offered his life if he converted to Islam. He refused and was executed. A monument to him stands at Thermopylae, near the monument to Leonidas.
