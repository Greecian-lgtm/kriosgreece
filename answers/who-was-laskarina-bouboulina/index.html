Who was Laskarina Bouboulina?

Laskarina Bouboulina was a shipowner from Spetses and a naval commander in the Greek War of Independence of 1821. After the deaths of her two husbands she managed their fortune and fleet herself.

She had the warship Agamemnon built and commanded it in the blockade of Nafplio. She was even arrested for a time by the revolutionary leadership, her own side, before being released.
